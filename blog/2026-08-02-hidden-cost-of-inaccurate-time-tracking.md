Legal practice is inherently detail-oriented. However, when it comes to time tracking, the foundation of law firm revenue, precision can be compromised due to urgency, ingrained habits, or inefficient workflows.

It starts small: a five-minute call left unlogged, an hour rounded down, a day reconstructed from memory. But multiply that across a team, a practice group, a firm, and the impact isn't small at all.

## Small Errors, Big Impact

Most inaccuracies in legal time tracking come down to two things:

- Underreporting: missing short or fragmented work.
- Misreporting: reconstructing the day after the fact, often from memory.

The results?

- Revenue leakage: even a modest 3-5% underreporting rate across a firm adds up fast.
- Weakened forecasting: inaccurate time data skews project scopes and resource planning.
- Client scepticism: vague or inconsistent entries can undermine confidence and trust.
